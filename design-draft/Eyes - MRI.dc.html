Authentication
The authentication subsystem issues short-lived access tokens bound to a device session. Tokens are minted by auth/token.rs and refreshed transparently against the session store described in ADR-014.
auth-login.png
Refresh occurs when a token is within 120 seconds of expiry. Failed refreshes invalidate the session and require re-authentication.
